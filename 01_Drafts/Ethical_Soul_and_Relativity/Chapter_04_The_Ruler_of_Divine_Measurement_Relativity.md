So let us make our definition here as clear, warm, and unmistakable as a sunny morning on the North Dakota prairie.   Lies, deception, cruelty, hatred, prejudice, manipulation, greed (*"All for Me"* extraction), and the moments when fear or wounded ego tempts a person to harm, deceive, or look down on another soul.
