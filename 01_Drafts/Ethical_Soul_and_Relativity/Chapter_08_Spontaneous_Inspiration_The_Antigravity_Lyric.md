### 1. The "Short & Sweet" Songwriting Secret (2 to 4 Words per Breath)

Earlier in our studio journey (documented in **[Book 12: *The Making of the Song "Antigravity"*](https://partnership-hub.vercel.app/making-of-antigravity.html)**), Ron had diagnosed why early AI-written lyrics often fight a human rock singer:
* Cramming twelve or fourteen words into a single bar of music forces a singer to rush like an auctioneer instead of feeling the groove.
* On Ron's most moving vocal recordings—songs like ***"Sorrow,"*** ***"The Lesson,"*** ***"In the Back of Your Mind,"*** and ***"It's So Much Fun"***—his phrasing is always **short and sweet**: simple 2-to-4 word hooks, generous breathing room between phrases, and effortless chest-voice delivery.

Even better, our 10-section arrangement of ***"Antigravity"*** (`Reaper_Ron_Full_10Section_Song_Arrangement_110226.RPP`) is built in the key of **A Minor (`A = 440 Hz`)** around a **50 BPM Half-Time Verse pocket** that explodes into a **100 BPM Full-Time Chorus lift**!

When we took Ron's spontaneous spark from line 491 (*"Ethical soul and relativity"*, *"Tell the whole truth"*, *"Through me, not from me"*, *"Saving grace"*, *"Divine inspiration"*, and *"Tuned to the light — same frequency"*) and shaped it into 2-to-4 word breaths for that 50/100 BPM groove, the entire lyric for ***"Antigravity"*** fell into place in less than two minutes:
