ANTIGRAVITY — VOCAL LYRIC SHEET ("THE LESSON" & "IN THE BACK OF YOUR MIND" PHRASING)
Key: A Minor / E Phrygian | Tempo: 120 BPM
================================================================================

HOW THIS PHRASING WORKS (JUST LIKE YOUR CLASSIC RECORDS):
1. VERSES ("In the Back of Your Mind" Style):
   - Sing low, warm, conversational chest voice (relaxed, right up on the mic).
   - Sing a short 4-to-5 word line on Beat 1, then STOP and let the Guitars & Organ breathe for 2 full seconds! Never rush or crowd the words.
2. CHORUSES ("The Lesson" Style — like "It's too late, it's too late"):
   - Jump up into your punchy upper register on short, repeating 3-word hooks!
   - The stacked Soundtoys-style cloned harmonies answer you in the gaps.

================================================================================
