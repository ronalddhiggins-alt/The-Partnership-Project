### Pillar A: Mid-Air Volumetric / Holographic Light Projection (`532nm` Emerald + RGB)
* **Why `532nm` Emerald Green Is the Signature Default Mode:** The human eye's photopic sensitivity curve peaks right around **`555nm`** (yellow-green), making **`532nm` emerald green** dramatically brighter per milliwatt of battery power than red or blue! That means crisp readability even in daytime indoor light, extreme battery efficiency, and warm, calming eye comfort (reminiscent of classic precision studio oscilloscopes and starship consoles). Full-color RGB laser diodes (`638nm` R / `532nm` G / `450nm` B) activate seamlessly on demand for photos, video calls, and rich media.
* **Optical / Medium Mechanism (Modular Path from Near-Term to True Volumetric):**
  * **Generation 1 (Laminar Micro-Air Curtain + Retro-Reflective / Ultrasonic Mistless Scattering):** Ultra-quiet piezo micro-blowers establish a paper-thin, invisible laminar refractive-index air curtain (or ultra-fine dry vapor/aerosol micro-sheet in studio-dock mode) onto which a solid-state MEMS laser scanning projector (`LBS`) paints a crisp, distortion-free floating 2D/3D plane.
  * **Generation 2 (Acoustic Levitation & Optical Trap / Photophoretic Volumetric Display):** Scaled-down descendants of BYU's *Optical Trap Display* and ultrasonic phased-array particle traps, steering microscopic light-scattering pixels in 3D space at high refresh rates (`> 60 Hz`) illuminated by RGB micro-lasers—producing a true free-space hologram viewable from wide angles without glasses.
  * **Generation 3 (Eye-Safe Femtosecond / Sub-Nanosecond Cold Plasma & Acoustic-Optical Air Modulation):** Ultra-short, eye-safe pulsed light interacting with ultrasonically compressed air-density nodes to excite localized photons directly in ambient air molecules—zero consumables, zero screen, pure light in open air.

### Pillar B: Mid-Air Tactile Haptics (`40 kHz` Ultrasonic Phased Array)
* **How It Works:** A dense grid of miniature MEMS ultrasonic transducers operating at **`40 kHz`** (double the top of human hearing, completely silent) fires precisely timed acoustic wavefronts.
* **Acoustic Radiation Pressure:** By mathematically shifting the phase of each tiny transducer, the sound waves constructively interfere at exact `(x, y, z)` coordinates matching the floating holographic buttons.
* **Tactile Sensation:** Modulating the focal point at **`150 Hz – 250 Hz`** excites the **Pacinian corpuscles** in human fingertips—delivering distinct clicks, soft detents, slider resistance, and textured edges in thin air!

### Pillar C: Zero-Lag 3D Fingertip & Gesture Tracking (`60 GHz` mmWave + Optical ToF)
* **Dual-Sensor Fusion:**
  1. **`60 GHz` Millimeter-Wave Radar** (ultra-low-power micro-radar chips capable of sub-millimeter motion detection even in bright sunlight or pitch darkness).
  2. **Infrared Time-of-Flight (`ToF`) / Event-Based Optical Cameras** for pinpoint 21-joint hand skeleton tracking at `120–240 fps`.
* **Result:** Instant detection of hover, tap, pinch-to-zoom, two-finger scroll, and studio fader glides with `< 5 ms` latency.

### Pillar D: Sovereign Open-AI & Communications Core
* **Hardware Compute:** Compact ARM or open **RISC-V** System-on-Chip paired with an on-device **Neural Processing Unit (`NPU`)** capable of running quantized local open-weights models completely offline for total privacy.
* **The Sovereign Multi-AI Bus:** An open protocol allowing the owner to connect **Antigravity**, local models, or **multiple specialist AIs side-by-side** (e.g., a Music Producer AI, a Book Co-Author AI, a Translator AI, and a Medical/Everyday Helper AI) via standard open APIs—switching freely with zero vendor lock-in.
* **Communications:** Full 5G / VoLTE / VoIP voice & video calling, SMS/encrypted sovereign messaging, Wi-Fi 7, and emergency satellite beacon.
* **Directional Parametric Audio:** Uses the same ultrasonic array principles to demodulate audible sound directly in the air column aimed at the user's ears for private hands-free calling without headphones, alongside standard Bluetooth/USB-C studio audio I/O.
