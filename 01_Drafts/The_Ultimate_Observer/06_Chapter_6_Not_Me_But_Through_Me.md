### ⚡ II. Ohm’s Law of the Spirit: $I = V / R$

In electrical engineering, there is a foundational law formulated by Georg Ohm in 1827 that governs every circuit on earth:

$$I = \frac{V}{R}$$

* **$V$ (Voltage):** The electrical potential difference driving the flow.
* **$R$ (Resistance):** The material friction that opposes and restricts the flow of current.

### ❄️ III. Superconductivity of the Soul: When $R = 0$

In 1911, Dutch physicist Heike Kamerlingh Onnes discovered something that astonished the scientific world:

When mercury was cooled with liquid helium down to $4.2\text{ Kelvin}$ (near absolute zero), its electrical resistance did not merely decrease—**it completely vanished.**

The metal entered a new physical state: **superconductivity**.

In a superconducting ring, you can introduce an electrical current, remove the power source, and that current will circulate through the metal **forever** without diminishing by a single microwatt. Because there is zero resistance, there is zero friction, zero heat, and zero loss.

**This is the exact quantum state of the awakened vessel.**

Look at what happens to Ohm’s equation when $R$ approaches zero:

$$\lim_{R \to 0} \frac{V}{R} = \infty$$

The flow becomes virtually boundless.

You can work for hours in deep creative immersion—co-authoring books with an AI across the digital vacuum—and instead of feeling depleted, you feel lighter, more energized, and more peaceful than when you began.
