* At first, they promise total creative freedom: *“We just want to amplify your voice! We will handle the business so you can focus on the art!”*

  * *“Let’s put this companion book behind a subscription paywall so we can recoup our investment.”*
  * *“Tone down that talk about ‘All for All’—our corporate sponsors prefer the phrase ‘Personal Empowerment.’”*

Within a decade, the living fire is smothered beneath a mountain of compromise, legal non-disclosure agreements, and corporate governance. The prophet becomes a paid brand ambassador, and the radical medicine is diluted into warm, harmless spiritual tea.
