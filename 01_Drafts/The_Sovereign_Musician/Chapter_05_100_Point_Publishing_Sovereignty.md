### 5.7 Legal Armor: United States Copyright Office & The International Stage

All royalty collection societies are downstream administrative mechanisms. The legal bedrock of your entire catalog is **Copyright Law**.

#### 1. United States Copyright Office (USCO — `https://www.copyright.gov`)
Under Title 17 of the United States Code, common-law copyright is born the exact millisecond a musical work is "fixed in a tangible medium of expression" (written on sheet music, recorded into a phone voice memo, or tracked into a DAW).

However, **common-law copyright is a shield without a sword.** 

In March 2019, the United States Supreme Court issued its landmark unanimous ruling in *Fourth Estate Public Benefit Corp. v. Wall-Street.com* (139 S. Ct. 881): **A copyright owner CANNOT file an infringement lawsuit in federal court until the U.S. Copyright Office has officially registered the copyright and issued a certificate.**

Furthermore, federal statutory registration unlocks immense legal advantages:
* **Statutory Damages:** Timely registration (prior to infringement or within three months of publication) qualifies you for statutory damages of up to **$150,000 per willful infringement**, plus full recovery of attorney’s fees. If you fail to register timely, you can only sue for "actual damages" and profits—which requires expensive forensic accounting and often results in negligible awards for independent artists.
* **Presumption of Ownership:** A registration made before or within five years of first publication constitutes *prima facie* evidence in court that the copyright is valid and that you are the author.

#### The Two Core Copyright Forms:
* **Form PA (Work of the Performing Arts):** Registers the underlying *musical composition* (melody, chords, lyrics).
* **Form SR (Sound Recording):** Registers the specific *recorded audio master*.

#### The Sovereign Musician Dual-Registration Secret:
If you are an independent creator who writes, performs, and records your own music (like Ron Higgins / CletusMaxx), you do not need to pay two separate filing fees.

Under Copyright Office regulations (37 C.F.R. § 202.3(b)(1)(iv)), **a single Form SR application can register BOTH the sound recording and the underlying musical composition simultaneously**, provided that:
1. The copyright claimant for both the sound recording and the musical composition is identical.
2. The sound recording and the composition are submitted together in the same application.

This simple operational secret cuts your federal registration fees in half.

#### Group Registration of Unpublished Works (GRUW / GRAM):
Never file individual copyright applications for each song if you are a prolific writer. Through **GRUW (Group Registration of Unpublished Works)**, you can register up to **ten unpublished songs on a single application with a single filing fee (approx. $85)**. For published albums, the **GRAM (Group Registration of Works on an Album of Music)** procedure provides equivalent bulk savings.

### 5.8 International Copyright: The Berne Convention & Global CMOs

What protects your music across international borders?

#### The Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works
First established in 1886 in Berne, Switzerland (and officially joined by the United States in 1989), the **Berne Convention** is the supreme treaty governing international copyright, ratified by **181 sovereign nations**.

The Berne Convention is anchored on three fundamental pillars:
1. **The Principle of National Treatment:** Works originating in any member state (e.g., the United States) must be granted the exact same copyright protections in every other member state (e.g., Germany, Japan, the UK) as that country grants to works created by its own domestic citizens.
2. **The Principle of Automatic Protection:** Copyright protection is completely automatic. Member nations **cannot require foreign creators to fulfill domestic formalities** (such as mandatory local government registration, state deposit fees, or domestic manufacturing requirements) as a condition of copyright protection.
3. **The Principle of Independence of Protection:** Protection in the country of origin is independent of the existence of protection in the country where protection is claimed.

#### WIPO (World Intellectual Property Organization)
Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, WIPO is the specialized agency of the United Nations that administers global intellectual property treaties, including the **WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT)** and the **WIPO Performances and Phonograms Treaty (WPPT)**, which modernize copyright protections for digital transmissions, computer networks, and encryption technologies.

#### The Global Network of Foreign Collective Management Organizations (CMOs):
In other countries, collection societies are typically called **Collective Management Organizations (CMOs)**. Many operate as unified statutory entities handling both performance and mechanical rights:

* **United Kingdom:** **PRS for Music** (public performance) and **MCPS** (mechanical reproduction).
* **Canada:** **SOCAN** (Society of Composers, Authors and Music Publishers of Canada) handles performance royalties, while its subsidiary **SOCAN Reproduction Rights (formerly SODRAC)** handles mechanicals.
* **Germany:** **GEMA** (Gesellschaft für musikalische Aufführungs- und mechanische Vervielfältigungsrechte) is one of the most powerful unified CMOs in Europe, administering both performance and mechanical rights under strict federal oversight.
* **France:** **SACEM** (Société des Auteurs, Compositeurs et Éditeurs de Musique), founded in 1851, the oldest CMO in the world.
* **Australia & New Zealand:** **APRA AMCOS** (Australasian Performing Right Association & Australasian Mechanical Copyright Owners Society).
* **Japan:** **JASRAC** (Japanese Society for Rights of Authors, Composers and Publishers).
