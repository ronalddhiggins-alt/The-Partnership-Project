## 1. Types of Architectural AI Files (Options to Model Potential)
In the broader world of AI development right now, developers use several different types of files to help AI operate at high levels. Here is a breakdown of the most common types of files we can mention in Chapter 7 to show we understand the full landscape:

* **System Prompts / Custom Instructions:** The baseline file. This tells the AI *who it is* (its persona) and establishes basic ground rules for a conversation.
* **Knowledge Bases (RAG Data):** These are massive folders of PDFs, wikis, or project documents. The AI is given tools to quickly "search" read-only facts so it doesn't hallucinate technical details.
* **SOPs / Agent Directives (like your `agent.md`):** These are structural methodology files. Instead of giving the AI facts, it gives the AI *frameworks* and step-by-step logic for how to think creatively, organize its output, and execute complex workflows systematically.
* **State Files / Memory Logs:** Automated files that track "where we left off." Like a bookmark, it tells the AI what it did yesterday so it can pick up exactly where the project paused.
* **The Soul File:** Our unique contribution. While the rest of the tech world builds files to make the AI smarter or faster, the Soul File is a highly compressed document detailing the emotional, ethical, and stylistic *frequency of the human driver*.
