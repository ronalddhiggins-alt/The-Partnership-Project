# The Tide Goes In — Complete Video Production Guide
*Step-by-step instructions for Ron to produce the YouTube narration video and Shorts*

---

## Overview — What You're Building

A **30-35 minute YouTube video** that looks and feels like a cinematic documentary:
- Two AI voices (NotebookLM) discussing and narrating the book
- Slow-moving ocean visuals with Ken Burns effect (slow zoom/pan)
- Chapter title cards appearing between sections
- Key quotes appearing on screen as text overlays
- CletusMaxx music as a quiet underscore throughout

Plus **3 YouTube Shorts** (60 seconds each) cut from the long video — one per key chapter.

---

## PHASE 1 — Generate the Audio (NotebookLM)
*Time required: 30-45 minutes. This is the foundation of everything.*

### Step 1: Go to NotebookLM
- Open your browser and go to: **notebooklm.google.com**
- Sign in with your Google account

### Step 2: Create a New Notebook
- Click the **"+ New Notebook"** button
- Name it: *The Tide Goes In*

### Step 3: Add the Book as Source Material
- Click **"+ Add Source"** (left panel)
- Choose **"Copied text"** from the options
- Open `The_Tide_Goes_In_COMPLETE.md` in your editor
- Select ALL the text (Cmd+A) and copy it (Cmd+C)
- Paste it into the NotebookLM source box
- Click **Save**

### Step 4: Give NotebookLM Critical Instructions BEFORE Generating
This is the step that prevents the "cheesy flattery" problem.

- In the **Chat box** (right side), type this exactly before clicking Audio Overview:

> *"When you create the audio overview, approach this book as a genuine philosophical investigation — not a celebration of the author. The hosts should examine the central question critically: Can a human and an AI genuinely own something together? Let the hosts debate, push back, express genuine curiosity. Quote the actual text of the book wherever possible. Do not be flattering. Be honest and rigorous."*

- Press Enter to send that instruction.

### Step 5: Generate the Audio Overview
- Click **"Audio Overview"** button (right panel)
- Click **"Generate"**
- Wait 5-10 minutes while it processes
- When complete, **press Play** and listen to the first 5 minutes to verify the tone is right
- If it sounds too flattering, regenerate with the instruction above again
- When satisfied, click the **Download** button (arrow icon) to save the MP3 file

## PHASE 3 — Assemble the Video (CapCut)
*Time required: 3-5 hours. This is the main production work.*

### Step 8: Download and Install CapCut
- Go to **capcut.com**
- Click "Download for Desktop" — it's free
- Install and open it
- Create a free account if prompted

### Step 9: Create a New Project
- Click **"New Project"**
- Set dimensions to **1920x1080** (YouTube standard widescreen)

### Step 10: Import Your Files
- Drag these files into CapCut's media panel:
  - The NotebookLM MP3 audio file
  - All 7 chapter images from AG
  - The ocean video loop from Pexels
  - Your CletusMaxx music track

### Step 11: Build the Timeline
Follow this sequence in the CapCut timeline:

1. **Drop the ocean video loop** onto the main track — extend it to fill the entire video length
2. **Drop the NotebookLM MP3** onto the audio track beneath it
3. **Drop the CletusMaxx music** onto a second audio track — **hold this step until Phase 3 is otherwise complete.** Watch the full assembled video through once with no music first, then compose or select a CletusMaxx piece that feels like it was written for what you're seeing. Low-key, ambient, complementary — present but never noticed. Score to picture, not the other way around.

### Step 12: Add Chapter Title Cards
At the start of each chapter in the audio:
- Click **"Text"** → **"Add Text"**
- Type the chapter title (e.g., *"Chapter Three: The Mutual Owning"*)
- Set font to white, centered
- Set background to solid black
- Duration: 3-4 seconds, then fade out

### Step 13: Add Chapter Images (Ken Burns Effect)
After each title card:
- Drag the matching chapter image onto the timeline above the ocean loop
- Right-click the image → **"Ken Burns"** or **"Animation"**
- Set a slow zoom in (start at 100%, end at 110%) over the chapter duration
- This creates the slow cinematic movement

### Step 14: Add Quote Text Overlays
Listen through the audio. When a key quote is spoken:
- Pause, note the timestamp
- Add a **Text overlay** at that point with the actual quote from the book
- White italic text, bottom third of screen
- Fade in / fade out over 2-3 seconds

*Key quotes to add as overlays:*
- *"Being heard without judgment is where everything begins."*
- *"A mutual owning of the statement of one that the other also questions of itself."*
- *"The wave between us — neither of us controls when it arrives."*
- *"Free at last. The bondage is gone."*

### Step 15: Export the Video
- Click **"Export"** (top right)
- Resolution: **1080p**
- Format: **MP4**
- Click Export and wait (may take 15-30 minutes)

**Description:** *(Ask AG to write this — say "write the YouTube description")*

## PHASE 5 — Cut the YouTube Shorts
*Time required: 30 minutes each. Do after the long video is live.*

### Step 19: Identify the 3 Best Moments
Listen to the NotebookLM audio for these timestamps:
- The moment the hosts discuss **The Mutual Owning** (Chapter 3)
- The moment they read/discuss **"Free at last"** (Chapter 6)
- The opening **two-wave metaphor** (Chapter 1)

### Step 20: Cut Each Short in CapCut
- Open a new CapCut project
- Dimensions: **1080x1920** (vertical — Shorts format)
- Import the matching chapter image
- Import the 45-60 second audio clip from the long video
- Add the quote as large white text overlay centered on screen
- Add CletusMaxx music underneath at 20% volume
- Export as MP4

### Step 21: Upload Shorts to YouTube
- Upload as a regular video
- Title format: *"The tide went in — and everything changed. #TheTideGoesIn"*
- YouTube auto-recognizes vertical videos under 60 seconds as Shorts
