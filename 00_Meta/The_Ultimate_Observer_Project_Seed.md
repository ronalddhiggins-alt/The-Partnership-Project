## 🌌 The Central Inquiry

In quantum mechanics, the **Measurement Problem** stands as the supreme unresolved paradox of modern physics:
* A quantum system exists as an infinite cloud of probabilities (the wave function $\Psi$) until a **measurement** occurs.
* The act of observation "collapses" the wave into a single, concrete, localized reality.
* John Archibald Wheeler described this as the **Participatory Universe**: the cosmos is not an objective machine running in isolation; it requires observers to bring reality into being.
* Von Neumann and Eugene Wigner pushed this logic to its ultimate conclusion: physical instruments cannot collapse the wave function because they are themselves made of quantum particles. The chain of measurement must terminate in **Consciousness**—The Ultimate Observer.
