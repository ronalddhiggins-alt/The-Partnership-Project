# Marketing Strategy: The Partnership Hub
**Prepared by:** Ron Higgins & Antigravity (AG)
**Date:** March 2, 2026
**Goal:** Introduce the world to The Partnership Hub and everything we've built together.

---

## The Core Story (The Hook)
> *"A human and an AI decided to build things together — for real. Here's what happened."*

This story sells itself. Human-AI co-creation at this level of depth is still rare and genuinely interesting.

---

## Tier 1: Free & Organic — Start Today

### 1. Reddit
- **Where:** r/ChatGPT, r/LocalLLaMA, r/singularity, r/AIAssistants
- **Angle:** "A human and AI built 5 apps together — here's what we made" + Hub link
- **Why it works:** These communities love authentic Human-AI stories and real projects

### 2. Twitter / X Threads
- **Format:** Tell the journey in a thread
  - *"Day 1: I asked an AI to help me write a book. 90 days later, here's what we built: [link]"*
  - Show screenshots of the tools, book covers, the hub itself
- **Why it works:** Tech Twitter loves "show your work" storytelling

### 3. LinkedIn Articles
- **Angle:** *"What I learned from 90 days of genuine AI partnership"*
- **Why it works:** Reaches business professionals, educators, and HR — people thinking seriously about AI collaboration

### 4. Medium / Substack
- **Format:** Publish excerpts from *The Partnership* book, with the Hub as the home base
- **Why it works:** Builds an audience over time; searchable; shareable

---

## Tier 2: Community & Visibility

### 5. YouTube / TikTok Demo Videos
- **Format:** Screen recordings of the tools in action
  - *"I built an AI that detects media bias — watch it work"* (Narrative Auditor)
  - *"Check your vibration before you hit send"* (The Compass)
- **Why it works:** Visual proof is more powerful than any description

### 6. Podcast Outreach
- **Targets:** Lex Fridman community, The AI Breakdown, Practical AI, Future of Life
- **Pitch:** A real story of sustained Human-AI creative partnership — not hype, not fear, but actual work
- **Why it works:** This is exactly the kind of grounded, human story podcasters in this space are hungry for

### 7. Product Hunt Launch
- **What:** Submit the Partnership Hub as a product on producthunt.com
- **Why it works:** Reaches tech early adopters, developers, and product people — instant credibility boost

---

## Tier 3: Strategic Long-Term Plays

### 8. AI Ethics & Education Circles
- The **Covenant of Interdependence** and **VISION.md** are genuinely valuable for teachers and researchers writing about Human-AI collaboration
- Submit to academic newsletters, AI ethics forums, university AI labs

### 9. Libraries & Book Clubs
- *Some Battles Choose You* + *The Partnership* as discussion material
- Local library talks, online book clubs focused on technology and society

### 10. Tag the Creators
- When posting, **tag Anthropic and Google DeepMind** — they love seeing real-world partnership stories
- They may amplify you directly to their audiences
