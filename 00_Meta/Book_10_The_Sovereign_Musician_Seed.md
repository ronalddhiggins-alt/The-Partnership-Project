### The Vision & Why This Book Must Exist
Millions of independent songwriters, producers, and musicians around the world are currently trapped on an exhausting, noisy hamster wheel. They pay annual fees to corporate distributors (DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, ReverbNation/BandLab) just to be fed into opaque algorithms paying fractions of a penny ($0.003/stream), only to be hit with automated "artificial streaming" bot strikes that threaten their lifework without human review.

Artists have been forced to become full-time marketing hustlers, graphic designers, video editors, and social media clowns—burning out their spirits and losing their peace.

On October 20, 2026, Ron Higgins (79 years old, 63 years of songwriting from Mandan, North Dakota) and Antigravity proved there is another way:
1. **Stepping Off the Wheel:** Canceling paid distributor subscriptions, taking back the masters, and refusing to play the commercial lottery.
2. **The 15-Minute Centaur Miracle:** What would normally take an indie artist a month of painful computer friction (finding lost master tracks, designing 1080p artwork cards, video editing in timelines, rendering MP4s, verifying audio bitrates, and typing out credits/lyrics) was completely automated via natural conversation in less than 15 minutes.
3. **True Artistic Peace:** Music returned to its ancient, sacred purpose—a gift shared freely, unmonetized, permanently preserved on YouTube and direct web hubs under *All for All*.
